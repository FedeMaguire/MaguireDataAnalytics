FlowLayerPRO

Real-time order flow analytics desktop app for crypto futures traders

A Windows desktop app that streams live trades and order-book depth from Binance, Bybit, and OKX, showing liquidity walls, large trades, icebergs, and delta at a 300 ms refresh rate. Built with a Python engine, a Tauri shell, and Plotly.js WebGL charts, developed with an AI coding assistant under strict review: every change was verified with logs or live-market stress tests (up to 4 h 41 min across three exchanges with zero slow cycles). It grew out of my earlier Binance trading bot with a Flask live dashboard.

CARMAGNO

CRM for car dealerships, with an insurance-quoting module

A web platform that runs a car dealership from one place: vehicle inventory, clients and leads, sales, commissions, vehicle-registration paperwork, and insurance quotes. Each agency's data is kept separate, with a shared inventory every agency can browse, and access is set by role for five user types. I worked as consultant and collaborator for the dealership owner: I turned his business needs into specs and instructions for an AI coding agent, and oversaw its work until each feature worked as he intended. Built with PHP, MySQL, and JavaScript on an 85-table database, and live since 2026.
