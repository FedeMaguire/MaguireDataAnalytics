CNCstudio

3D product design, CNC, and CAD/CAM

History

Founder and lead designer at CNCstudio, a precision-focused CNC solutions business established in 2010. It served the specific cutting needs of the sports boat and furniture industries. Our goal was to bring a blend of creativity and precision to every project, ensuring high-quality results for our clients.

My skills grew across 3D design tools, 3D printing, G-code programming, Mach3, Rhino, CAD/CAM, Arduino, analytical skills, problem-solving, and teamwork.
