CNC STUDIO

3D PRODUCT DESIGN / CNC / CAD-CAM

history

Founder and lead designer at CNCstudio, a precision-focused CNC solutions business established in 2010. It served the specific cutting needs of the sports boat and furniture industries. Our goal was to bring a blend of creativity and precision to every project, ensuring high-quality results for our clients.
My skills grew across 3D design tools, 3D printing, G-code programming, Mach3, Rhino, CAD/CAM, Arduino, analytical skills, problem-solving, and teamwork.

clients

At Housestyle, our brand specialized in CNC-designed products, my focus was on balancing quality and cost in our production process. At CNCstudio we maintained a commitment to craftsmanship. I supervised design, production, final assembly, and refinements at our factory, ensuring each piece met our standards.

  • visit Housestyle

At Lanchas Bermuda, CNCstudio digitized and organized handcrafted structural plywood templates for precision CNC cutting. Each piece was categorized by boat model and part, then went through a streamlined production process to ensure it met specific standards.

  • visit Lanchas Bermuda

At Lampmakers, a brand devoted to CNC flat-cut lamps made from specialized plastics, I handled design, production, and photography, improving product quality and cost efficiency. Final assembly and refinements at our factory ensured attention to detail in every phase of production.

  • visit Lampmakers

For COVID-19 safety masks made from specialized plastics, I handled design, production, and photography, improving both product quality and cost efficiency. Final assembly and refinements were carried out at our factory, with attention to detail at every stage of production.
